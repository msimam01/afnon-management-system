About NECAS

The Nigerian Emergency Commodity Assistance Scheme (NECAS) is a government initiative designed to support smallholder farmers with access to quality agricultural inputs and financial assistance.

Our Impact

Over 500,000 farmers supported across Nigeria

₦50 billion in agricultural loans distributed

2.5 million hectares of farmland improved

95% loan repayment rate through commodity returns

How It Works

Getting support from NECAS is simple and straightforward. Follow these steps to access agricultural grants and loans.

1

Register & Apply

Create your farmer profile with BVN, NIN, and farm details. Submit your application for the current season.

2

Get Approved

Our team reviews your application and farm size to calculate your commodity allocation. Receive SMS notification upon approval.

3

Collect & Farm

Visit your assigned collection center to receive seeds, fertilizers, and other inputs. Start farming with confidence.

4

Harvest & Return

After harvest, return 110% of received commodities or pay cash equivalent with 5% interest to complete the cycle.

5

Continue Growing

Successful farmers can apply for larger allocations in subsequent seasons and access additional support programs.

Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for NECAS support, farmers must meet the following requirements:

Basic Requirements

Nigerian citizen with valid NIN

Valid Bank Verification Number (BVN)

Age between 18-65 years

Active phone number for SMS notifications

Farm Requirements

Minimum 0.5 hectares of farmland

Located in participating states and clusters

Commitment to seasonal farming activities

No outstanding debt from previous seasons

Important Note

Applications are reviewed on a first-come, first-served basis within each cluster. Ensure all required documents are uploaded during registration to avoid delays in processing.
